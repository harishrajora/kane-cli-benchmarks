## Severity levels

| Level | Example | Response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEV1 | Production down for all customers | 15 minutes |
| SEV2 | A major feature is broken | 1 hour |
| SEV3 | A minor bug with a workaround | Next business day |

## Steps

1. Acknowledge the page.
2. Assign an incident lead.
3. Post updates every 30 minutes for SEV1 and SEV2.
4. Write a post-incident review within 5 business days.
