Welcome to Northwind! This page covers your first day.

## Day one

1. Collect your laptop from the IT desk on floor 3.
2. Sign in to your email and set up two-factor authentication.
3. Meet your onboarding buddy at 11:00.
4. Join the `#welcome` channel and introduce yourself.

## Your first week

Work through the [Accounts and Tools](page:onboarding/accounts-and-tools) page, then read the [Codebase Tour](page:onboarding/codebase-tour).

Questions? Ask your buddy or email people@northwind.example.
